# LLRD brand system

The locked identity is LLRD Technologies: “Technology that connects what matters.” Pillars: People, Possibilities, Progress, Purpose. Deep green, sunrise gold, stone, sand and ivory form the palette. Status colors are independent semantic tokens; visible focus and readable contrast take precedence.

Master logo, star, Earth/sunrise artwork, product artwork, favicon and social images have not been supplied. Never redraw these as purported final assets. The initial site uses a plain-text company identifier and a deliberately typographic hero. `content/brand.ts` is the replacement manifest; each asset folder is independent. Do not interpret empty paths as broken image sources. Exact artwork and approved alternative text can be inserted without editing page layouts.

Typography uses local system sans-serif and Georgia to avoid third-party font requests. All primary controls target at least 44px. Motion respects reduced-motion preferences.
