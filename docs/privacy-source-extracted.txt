PART II — U.S. STATE PRIVACY RIGHTS

16. TENNESSEE PRIVACY

LLRD is organized in Tennessee and intends to maintain privacy practices consistent with applicable Tennessee requirements.

The Tennessee Information Protection Act (“TIPA”) applies only when its statutory applicability requirements and thresholds are satisfied. Among other requirements, the Act generally applies to persons conducting business in Tennessee or producing products or services targeted to Tennessee residents that exceed the statutory revenue threshold and meet specified consumer-data processing thresholds. LLRD's organization or location in Tennessee, by itself, does not mean that TIPA applies to every LLRD activity.

Where TIPA applies, qualifying Tennessee consumers may have rights that include the ability to:

confirm whether personal information is being processed;
access personal information;
correct inaccuracies;
request deletion;
obtain certain information in a portable format;
opt out of qualifying sales of personal information;
opt out of targeted advertising;
opt out of certain profiling; and
appeal certain decisions concerning privacy requests.

LLRD may voluntarily provide some privacy controls even where a particular statutory threshold has not been met. The availability of a voluntary control does not constitute a representation that a particular privacy statute applies to LLRD or to a particular processing activity.
